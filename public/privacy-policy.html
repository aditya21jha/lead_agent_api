Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Royal Hair Istanbul respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you communicate with Royal Hair Istanbul through our website, WhatsApp, advertising channels, customer-support channels or related business tools.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact information such as your name, telephone number, email address and country.
  • Communication information, including messages and information you choose to send to us through WhatsApp or other communication channels.
  • Images or photographs that you voluntarily send to us for the purpose of requesting a hair-treatment or hair-transplant consultation.
  • Lead and appointment information, such as your enquiry, requested service, preferred contact method and communication history.
  • Technical information that may be provided by your browser or the services through which you interact with us.

2. How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, provide requested consultations and medical-evaluation assistance, communicate about requested services, arrange appointments, provide customer support, manage leads and customer communications, improve our services, and maintain the security and operation of our business systems.

3. WhatsApp and Meta services

When you communicate with us through WhatsApp, your messages and contact information may be processed through WhatsApp and Meta services used by Royal Hair Istanbul to manage business communications. We use these services to receive and respond to enquiries, send requested communications and manage customer conversations.

4. Photos and consultation information

If you voluntarily send photographs or other information for a consultation, we use that information for the requested evaluation and related customer communication. You should only send information that you are comfortable sharing for this purpose.

5. Sharing of information

We may use service providers and business technology platforms that process information on our behalf, including communication, customer relationship management, hosting and advertising platforms. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, systems or business.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including customer-service, business, legal and record-keeping requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to stop sending non-essential communications. If you no longer want to receive WhatsApp communications from us, you can reply to the relevant message with a request to stop or contact us directly.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Royal Hair Istanbul through the contact details published on our official website.
